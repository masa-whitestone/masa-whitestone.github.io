I worked on solving the elliptic-curve discrete logarithm problem with Shor's
algorithm during my time at BlocQ. An algorithm that is polynomial on paper can
still be far out of reach in qubit count, T-gate count and circuit depth, and
the gap between those two facts is where the engineering lives.

I am interested in implementations concrete enough to be counted — and in what
those counts imply for how much time cryptography actually has.
