At the University of Osaka's Quantum Software Research Hub I worked on loading
classical data into quantum circuits — a step that is easy to gloss over and
expensive enough to erase the speedup it was meant to enable.

At JIJ I looked at the other end of the same pipeline, applying quantum
computing to operations research and machine learning problems where the
question is whether a hybrid method beats a good classical baseline at all.
